Dynamic Webs corner asset validation
====================================

Generator version: 1.3.0
Master seed: dynamic-webs:web-corner-assets:v3
Topology: distributed-sheet
Angle families: 12
Variants per family: 6
Expected assets: 72

Visual topology
---------------
Corner assets contain no compulsory hub, radial spoke fan, capture rings or
authored knot. Wall supports, cross-corner scaffolds, local mesh edges and
staggered tethers distribute intersections throughout an irregular sheet.

Visibility guardrails
---------------------
Full-strength threshold angle: 20 degrees
Minimum narrow-angle factor: 0.8
Full minimum mean alpha: 1.7
Full minimum alpha > 64 coverage: 0.9%
Full minimum alpha > 128 coverage: 0.7%

Whole-canvas density is scaled modestly only below 20 degrees because acute
webs occupy a smaller wedge of the square source canvas. The 5-degree family
uses factor 0.85; 10 degrees uses 0.90; 15 degrees uses 0.95. Ordinary and
obtuse families retain the full thresholds.

The validator also requires generator version 1.3.0, the v3 master seed and
manifest topology "distributed-sheet", so an old hub-and-spoke asset set cannot
pass without being regenerated.

Generated WebPs are local outputs and are not included in replacement ZIPs.
